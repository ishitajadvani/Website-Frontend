Comprehensive Medical Services

Emergency Medicine

Round-the-clock emergency care with trauma specialists and state-of-the-art resuscitation facilities available 24 hours a day, every day.

Cardiology

Advanced cardiac care including interventional cardiology, echocardiography, and comprehensive heart disease prevention programs.

Oncology

Comprehensive cancer treatment with the latest chemotherapy, radiation therapy, and surgical oncology delivered by expert multidisciplinary teams.

Maternity & Women's Health

Dedicated women's health services from prenatal care through delivery, neonatal intensive care, and postnatal support with expert midwives.

Pediatrics

Specialized care for children from newborns to adolescents, including a dedicated PICU, developmental pediatrics, and pediatric surgery.

Radiology & Imaging

Full-spectrum diagnostic imaging including 3T MRI, CT, PET scans, ultrasound, and digital X-ray with rapid reporting by consultant radiologists.

Our Story

A Legacy of Healing
Since 1999

Founded in Abu Dhabi in 1999, Al Fajr Hospital has grown into one of the UAE's most trusted healthcare institutions. Our name — meaning "dawn" in Arabic — reflects our commitment to bringing hope, renewal, and the highest standard of care to every patient we serve.

  • JCI and DHA accredited facility meeting international healthcare standards
  • State-of-the-art surgical suites with robotic-assisted surgery capabilities
  • Multilingual staff serving Arabic, English, Hindi, and Tagalog speaking patients
  • Advanced electronic health records for seamless, coordinated care
  • Dedicated wellness and preventive medicine programs for the community
